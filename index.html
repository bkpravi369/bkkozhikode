Raja Yoga is the ancient art of open-eyed spiritual meditation. By gently remembering oneself as an immortal point of peaceful light at the center of the forehead, the soul connects directly with the Supreme Source of all virtues, Shiv Baba.

This practice restores clarity, emotional resilience, pure love, and effortless mastery over thoughts, speech, and actions in daily family and professional life.
